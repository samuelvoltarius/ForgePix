# Equipment catalogue provenance

Presets are starting values, not measured calibration or a camera whitelist.
Manual entries remain available. A sensor name alone cannot determine noise:
gain, readout mode, temperature, exposure and calibration also matter.

Sources checked 2026-09-05 for added presets:

- ZWO ASI2600MM: https://i.zwoastro.com/zwo-website/manuals/ZWO_ASI2600_Manual_EN.pdf
- ToupTek ATR2600C: https://touptek-astro.com/dl_manual/ATR2600C_en.pdf
- ToupTek ATR533C: https://www.touptek-astro.com/dl_manual/ATR533C_en.pdf
- ToupTek ATR533M: https://www.touptek-astro.com/blog/square-frame-easy-shooting-touptek-astro-atr533m-is-here
- QHY268C/M: https://www.qhyccd.com/astronomical-camera-qhy268/

These additions provide pixel pitch (3.76 micrometres). They do not supply
gain-dependent read noise, quantum efficiency or spectral response curves.
